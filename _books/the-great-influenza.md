Great read. The  story of the people and institutions responsible for the transformation of medicine in the US was fascinating. It's worth noting the parallels between the influenza and covid, particularly how the private-public precedent set in 1918 set the world up for a better response to the pandemic. I learned quite a bit about the cell structure of viruses, how they mutate and developed great admiration for research and the institutions that facilitate it.
