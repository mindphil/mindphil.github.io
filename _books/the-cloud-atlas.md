---
title: "The Cloud Atlas"
author: "Liam Callanan"
---

One of the best novels I've read (so far), Callanan has such a way of pulling you into the story. You join Louis Belk, stationed in Alaska during the waning days of WW2. The Yup'ik Eskimo traditions and metaphysical experiences, contrasted by the grim realities of the war make for an enthralling read.
